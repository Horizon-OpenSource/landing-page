THE CHALLENGE

Environmental Information Is Often Scattered Across Multiple Tools

Many organizations still manage environmental information using spreadsheets, independent documents and manual processes. This makes it harder to consolidate emissions data, keep information updated, compare results and prepare environmental reports.

EcoTrack brings this information together in one platform to support carbon footprint management and environmental analysis.

01

Centralized Environmental Data

Organize emissions information from different areas, processes and locations within a single platform.

02

Automated Carbon Footprint Calculations

Use registered emissions data and emission factors to obtain carbon footprint results with less dependence on manual calculations.

03

Clearer Monitoring and Reporting

Review indicators, compare environmental information over time and generate reports that support analysis and decision-making.
