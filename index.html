THE CHALLENGE

Environmental Information Is Often Scattered Across Multiple Tools

Many organizations still manage environmental information using spreadsheets, independent documents and manual processes. This makes it harder to consolidate emissions data, keep information updated, compare results and prepare environmental reports.

EcoTrack brings this information together in one platform to support carbon footprint management and environmental analysis.

01

Centralized Environmental Data

Organize emissions information from different areas, processes and locations within a single platform.

02

Automated Carbon Footprint Calculations

Use registered emissions data and emission factors to obtain carbon footprint results with less dependence on manual calculations.

03

Clearer Monitoring and Reporting

Review indicators, compare environmental information over time and generate reports that support analysis and decision-making.

WHO ECOTRACK IS FOR

Built for Organizations Managing Complex Environmental Information

EcoTrack is designed for organizations that need to organize, analyze and monitor greenhouse gas emissions and environmental information across their operations.

01

Exporting Companies

CORE CAPABILITIES

Everything You Need to Manage Your Carbon Footprint

EcoTrack centralizes the main processes involved in managing corporate greenhouse gas emissions, from data registration to environmental analysis and reporting.

01

Emissions Management

Register and classify emission sources across Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3, while maintaining an organized history of emissions data.

02

Carbon Footprint Calculation

Automatically calculate emissions using emission factors and review carbon footprint results by scope and reporting period.

03

Goals & Reduction Plans

Create environmental goals, define reduction plans, register initiatives and monitor progress toward emission reduction objectives.

04

Analytics & Reports

Explore dashboards, environmental indicators, period comparisons and major emission sources, and generate environmental reports.

05

Organization Management

Manage your organization, locations and users through structured access, roles and permissions designed for business environments.
